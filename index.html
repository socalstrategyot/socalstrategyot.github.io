The 2027 workshop

The workshop brings together faculty, postdocs, and doctoral students working in strategy and organization theory at Southern California institutions for a day of research presentations and unhurried conversation. It is deliberately small, informal, and free to attend.

Registration and the call for papers will open in Fall 2026. Add your email below and we'll send you the details as soon as they're set.
